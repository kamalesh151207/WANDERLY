OUR METHODOLOGY

The 5 Pillars of Travel Cost

We analyze historical pricing across flights, trains, boutique stays, local markets, and experience tickets to build accurate estimates.

01

Transport

Long-distance travel (flight/rail/road) + estimated local cabs or scooter rentals.

02

Accommodation

Daily room rates calculated for your chosen travel style (Budget, Balanced, Comfort, Luxury).

03

Food & Dining

3 daily meals plus snacks, bottled water, and regional culinary experiences.

04

Activities

Monument entry passes, national park permits, guided tours, and water sports.

05

Emergency Buffer

A recommended 10% safety margin for unexpected surge fees or shopping.
